Mapo Tofu

Description

Mapo tofu is a delicious Chinese dish. This dish has ground beef or pork, tofu, and is sauced down by a nice mild-medium schezuan peppery sauce. One of noobprogrammer67'a favorite recipes.

Ingredients

  • Tofu
  • Ground beef
  • Green onion
  • Garlic
  • Pre-made mapo sauce

Steps

  1. Mince garlic and cut and separate whites and green of onion
  2. Sear ground beef on side and then fully cook
  3. Add whites of onion and garlic to ground beef
  4. Add mapo sauce and tofu
  5. Simmer for 6-8 minutes
