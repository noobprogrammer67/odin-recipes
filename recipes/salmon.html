Salmon

Description

Miso glazed Salmon is a dish inspired by the miso black cod at Nobu. noobprogrammer67 tried to replicate it with Salmon. A salmon filet is marinaded in a white miso sauce for 24 hours, baked and finished with a broil.

Ingredients

  • White Miso
  • Mirin
  • Sake
  • Sugar
  • Salmon

Steps

  1. Make the marinade by boiling equal parts sake and mirin for 30 seconds to burn off the alcohol
  2. Add the white miso and sugar and mix until homogenous
  3. Let the marinade cool down
  4. Add the marinade to a piece of salmon and marinade for 24 hours
  5. After the salmon is done marinading, bake the salmong at 385 degrees Farenheit for 18 minutes and finish with a 1-2 minute broil
