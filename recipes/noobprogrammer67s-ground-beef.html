Ground Beef

Description

noobprogrammer67's ground beef is an Asian inspired ground beef dish. A sauce is made out of soy sauce, brown sugar, and hoison sauce that is poured over the ground beef after cooking it in a pan. It's clean and tastes kind of mid. It's best eaten for weight cutters.

Ingredients

  • 93/7 ground beef
  • Soy sauce
  • Brown sugar
  • Hoison sauce
  • Garlic
  • Green onion

Steps

  1. Mince garlic and cut green onions, make sure to separate the white and green parts
  2. Prepare sauce by adding soy sauce, brown sugar, and hoison sauce
  3. Add oil to a pan
  4. Sear ground beef on one side for 2-3 minutes
  5. Fully cook ground beef and add onion and garlic and the white parts of the green onion
  6. Pour sauce into pan and simmer for 2-3 minutes
  7. Serve and eat with rice
